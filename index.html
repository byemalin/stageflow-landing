Features

Everything your internship office needs

Automated agreement generation

Students submit their internship details once. StageFlow generates a compliant convention de stage PDF, pre-filled and formatted, ready to sign. No manual document editing.

DocuSign e-signature routing

All parties (student, employer, academic tutor, institution director) sign in the correct order, automatically. Full audit trail. No chasing. No lost emails.

Powered by DocuSign

Role-based dashboards

Students track their agreement status in real time. Coordinators see every internship at a glance. Employers confirm details online. Each role sees exactly what they need.

Automated email notifications

Every status change triggers the right email to the right person. Students know when to act. Employers know what is expected. Your team stays out of the loop until they need to be in it.

Reporting, charts, and CSV export

View live charts by cohort, employer, and status. Export a complete internship master list at any time, filterable by cohort, status, employer, or date range. Built for the reporting your institution already does.

Designed around the convention de stage

StageFlow is built for the internship agreement process, not adapted from a generic HR tool. The document template and workflow are configured to fit your institution's exact requirements.

Process

From student submission to signed agreement. Hours, not weeks.

01

Student submits

The student fills out internship details (employer, role, dates, supervisor) through a guided online form. No attachments, no PDFs, no back and forth.

02

Convention generated

StageFlow auto-generates a fully compliant convention de stage PDF. No manual editing. No copy-pasting between systems.

03

All parties sign

DocuSign routes the agreement to the student, employer, academic tutor, and institution director, in the right sequence, automatically.

04

Agreement archived

The fully signed convention is stored, searchable, and exportable. Every record in one place, permanently.

This flow is an example. Because StageFlow is deployed per institution, the entire process can be tailored to fit your programme's exact requirements: signing order, approval stages, required fields, notification logic, and more. You own the flow.
