From submission to signed agreement. Hours, not weeks.

One agreement, handed from one person to the next. Each person does their part once and StageFlow passes it on. No scanning, no printing, no chasing.

  1. 1Student

    Student submits

    The student fills out internship details (employer, role, dates, supervisor) through a guided online form. No attachments, no PDFs, no back and forth.

  2. 2Internship office

    Internship office approves

    The internship office reviews the request and approves it before anything goes out for signature.

  3. 3Employer

    Employer completes the details

    The employer adds their side (supervisor, tasks, hours, pay) and previews the convention de stage, generated automatically. No manual editing.

  4. 4All three

    All three parties sign

    DocuSign sends the convention to each signatory in order, automatically.

    Employer→Student→School

✓Convention complete. The fully signed agreement is recorded, searchable, and exportable. Every record in one place.

*This flow is an example. StageFlow is deployed per institution, so the process is set up around your programme: signing order, approval stages, required fields, notification logic, and more. You own the flow.

Common questions.

Are DocuSign signatures legally binding?

Yes. StageFlow uses DocuSign, which is eIDAS-compliant. Electronic signatures produced via DocuSign carry the same legal weight as handwritten signatures under EU law, including for the convention de stage.

Is the platform GDPR compliant?

StageFlow stores only the structured data required to generate and manage internship agreements. No PDF contracts are retained on our servers. Data hosting is configured per institution and handled in line with GDPR obligations.

How does onboarding work?

We start with a call or email exchange to understand your institution's process and requirements. From there we scope the setup together. Timeline depends on your needs and how quickly your team can be involved.

Can the workflow be adapted to our process?

Yes. Because StageFlow is deployed per institution, the approval stages, required fields, signing order, and notification logic are all configured to match your existing process. The goal is to fit your process, not the other way around.

Does it integrate with our student information system?

StageFlow currently supports CSV export for student and programme data, with direct integrations under development. If your institution uses a specific SIS, get in touch and we can discuss what is possible.

What support is included?

All subscriptions include setup support, configuration assistance, and ongoing access to the StageFlow team. During onboarding, we work directly with your internship office to ensure the platform is configured correctly and your staff are comfortable using it. For institutions with specific requirements, such as integration with existing systems or custom workflows, we're open to discussing what that could look like.
